Ph.D. Student Hiring

I am seeking highly motivated Ph.D. students interested in smart manufacturing, artificial intelligence, industrial IoT, intelligent sensing, and cyber-physical manufacturing systems.

Preferred background / skills (experience in one or more areas is welcome):

  • Programming & Software: Python, MATLAB, Linux, Git, SQL / NoSQL databases
  • AI, Data Analytics & Sensing: machine learning and deep learning frameworks (e.g., PyTorch, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn), signal processing (e.g., vision, sound, vibration), time-series analysis
  • Hardware & Experimental Systems: CNC machines, 3D printers, robot arms, edge computing devices (e.g., Raspberry Pi, NVIDIA Jetson), sensors and data acquisition systems
  • Manufacturing & Industrial Systems: smart manufacturing, industrial IoT, MTConnect, OPC UA, digital twins, manufacturing monitoring and automation

Students from mechanical engineering, manufacturing, electrical engineering, computer science, robotics, or related disciplines are encouraged to apply. Strong motivation, research curiosity, and willingness to learn are highly valued.

Collaborations

I welcome opportunities for academic, industry, and interdisciplinary collaborations related to smart manufacturing, AI-enabled engineering systems, industrial sensing, and digital manufacturing.
