Teaching Philosophy

My teaching emphasizes experiential learning, hands-on implementation, and the integration of engineering theory with real-world applications. I aim to help students build a strong theoretical foundation and develop practical skills in smart manufacturing, industrial IoT, artificial intelligence, data analytics, and engineering systems.
