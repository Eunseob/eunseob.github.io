About

I am an incoming Assistant Professor in Mechanical Engineering at Wayne State University. My research focuses on smart manufacturing, artificial intelligence, industrial IoT, multimodal sensing, and cyber-physical manufacturing systems.

Research Interests

  • AI-enabled smart manufacturing
  • Industrial IoT and cyber-physical manufacturing systems
  • Multimodal sensing and sensor data analytics
  • Machine learning for manufacturing monitoring and diagnostics
  • Acoustic and vibration-based process monitoring

Lab

I am establishing the Knowledge-based Intelligent Monitoring and Manufacturing (KIMM) Lab at Wayne State University.
