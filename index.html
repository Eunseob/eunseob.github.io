Biography

Eunseob Kim is an incoming Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Wayne State University. His research focuses on smart manufacturing, artificial intelligence, industrial IoT, multimodal sensing, and cyber-physical manufacturing systems, with applications in equipment monitoring, diagnostics, and industrial manufacturing systems.

About

I am an incoming Assistant Professor in Mechanical Engineering at Wayne State University. My research focuses on smart manufacturing, artificial intelligence, industrial IoT, multimodal sensing, and cyber-physical manufacturing systems.

My work integrates AI, sensing, industrial data systems, and manufacturing knowledge to develop intelligent monitoring, diagnostics, and cyber-physical manufacturing solutions.

Research Interests

  • AI and manufacturing knowledge integration
  • Industrial IoT and cyber-physical manufacturing systems
  • Multimodal sensing and sensor data analytics
  • Machine learning for manufacturing monitoring and diagnostics
  • Acoustic and vibration-based process monitoring

Lab

I am establishing the Knowledge-based Intelligent Monitoring and Manufacturing (KIMM) Lab at Wayne State University, focusing on the integration of AI, sensing, and manufacturing knowledge for intelligent industrial systems.
