Research Vision

My research aims to advance intelligent manufacturing systems that can sense, learn, monitor, and adapt to complex process conditions. I develop data-driven and physics-informed approaches that integrate multimodal sensing, machine learning, industrial IoT, and cyber-physical manufacturing systems.

Research Areas

  • AI-enabled smart manufacturing and process monitoring
  • Multimodal sensing using sound, vibration, temperature, current, and machine controller data
  • Industrial IoT, MTConnect, and manufacturing data pipelines
  • Machine learning and representation learning for sensor time-series data
  • Cyber-physical manufacturing systems and edge intelligence

KIMM Lab

I am establishing the Knowledge-based Intelligent Monitoring and Manufacturing (KIMM) Lab at Wayne State University.
